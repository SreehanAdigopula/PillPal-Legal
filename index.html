About PillPal

PillPal is an iOS medication reminder and reference application designed to help individuals organize their medication schedules, review dose adherence history, and look up publicly available drug label information from the U.S. Food & Drug Administration's OpenFDA database.

Platform: iOS (iPhone & iPad)  ·  Developer: Adigopula Solutions (Sreehan Adigopula)  ·  Distribution: United States App Store only  ·  Version: 1.0

PillPal is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. See the Medical Disclaimer below for full details.

🚨 Medical Emergency — Call 911

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 9-1-1 immediately. For Poison Control (U.S.) call 1-800-222-1222. Do NOT use PillPal to seek emergency help.

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 11, 2026  ·  Last Reviewed: August 11, 2026

📱

Local-First Design

PillPal is designed as a local-first application. Your medication records, dose history, reminder schedules, and adherence data are stored exclusively on your device. PillPal has no user account, no sign-in, and no server that stores your personal information.

🔒

Data Stored On Your Device Only

  • Medication names, optional brand names, and user-entered dose text
  • Reminder times and schedule configurations
  • Dose outcome history, recorded timestamps, missed-dose status, and adherence summaries
  • Snooze event history
  • Cached OpenFDA drug label snapshots
  • App lock, appearance, time zone, and reminder delivery preferences
🌐

Data Sent Off Your Device

PillPal transmits data off your device in only two limited circumstances:

  • Medication name text and optional brand name text — sent to the U.S. Food & Drug Administration's public OpenFDA API (api.fda.gov) only when you enable FDA Label Lookup and request a drug label check. This text is not linked to any user account, identifier, or personal profile.
  • Voice audio or transcription — sent to Apple's Speech Recognition service only if your device does not support on-device recognition and you choose to use the optional voice input feature. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Standard network metadata (such as IP address) may be visible to OpenFDA or Apple as part of the above requests. PillPal does not control this.
🚫

Data PillPal Does Not Collect

  • No user account, registration, or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK
  • No advertising SDK or ad network
  • No iCloud or CloudKit sync in this version
  • No caregiver or third-party data sharing
  • No access to Contacts, Photos, Calendar, Location, or HealthKit records
  • No device fingerprinting or cross-app tracking
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA)
✋

Permissions PillPal May Request

  • Notifications — to deliver medication reminders with sound and action buttons
  • Alarm permission — for system-style alarms on supported iOS 26.1+ devices; standard notifications used as fallback
  • Face ID / Device Authentication — for the optional app-lock feature; data stays on device
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — only when you explicitly choose voice input for medication entry

All permissions are optional except notifications, which are required for reminders to function. You can revoke any permission at any time in iOS Settings.

🗁

Third-Party Services

  • OpenFDA API (open.fda.gov) — U.S. Government public domain drug label data. Governed by FDA's terms of service. PillPal sends medication name text and optional brand name text; no user identity, dose history, schedule, device identifier, or location is transmitted.
  • Apple Speech Recognition — Optional; used only if you choose voice input and on-device recognition is unavailable. Governed by Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).
🗑

Data Deletion

You can permanently delete all PillPal data at any time via Settings → Delete Data → Delete All My Data. This removes medication records, dose history, snooze history, cached label checks, URL cache, and all pending local reminders from your device. This action cannot be undone.

You may also clear older history selectively via Settings → Clear History.

Data Retention: All data is retained on your device indefinitely until you choose to delete it via Settings. No data is stored on any external server by Adigopula Solutions.
